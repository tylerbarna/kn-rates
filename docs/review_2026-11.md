## 1. Critical scientific issues

### 1.1 No real-data search — the "limits" are sensitivity forecasts
`R = −ln(0.05)/⟨VT⟩` assumes zero real candidates passing *the same criteria*. The repository contains no
search of ZTF alerts/forced photometry, and the manuscript states "no kilonovae were discovered".

*Options*
- **A. Perform the archival search** (Kowalski/Fritz alerts + forced photometry) with exactly the simulated
  criteria, vet survivors, and use N_obs (0 or more; `knrates.stats.poisson_factor(cl, n_obs)` already handles
  N_obs > 0). *Implication:* the only route to a genuine observational limit; the largest piece of work.
- **B. Re-cast the paper as a sensitivity/efficiency study** ("expected limit if no candidates survive").
  *Implication:* publishable quickly, much weaker claim; no comparison with GW rates as a constraint.
- **C. Re-use published null searches:** re-simulate Andreoni+2020/2021 criteria (per-band fade 0.57/0.39/0.27,
  no |b| cut / |b|>10°) for their windows and combine VT. *Implication:* a real limit for 2018–2021 only, and
  requires matching their pipeline cuts.

### 1.2 Recovery efficiency is an upper bound on a real pipeline
The simulation applies photometric thresholds and cadence only. Real searches lose genuine transients to
real-bogus scores (drb/braai), PS1-star and Solar-System vetoes, bad subtractions, Galactic extinction, and
human vetting. Evidence: over Andreoni's 23-month window our injections give R₉₅ = 1069 (GW170817-like,
ι = 26°) vs. their 1775 — ~1.7× more sensitive, despite similar cuts.
*Options:* (a) multiply ε by a literature-based completeness with an uncertainty band (fast, systematics-dominated);
(b) model vetting losses explicitly in survey_sim (per-epoch real-bogus efficiency vs. S/N, extinction from SFD);
(c) at minimum quote the factor and its effect on R. *Implication:* limits weaken by 10–40 %, but become defensible.

### 1.3 Fabricated analyses and figures
- Suites 05 and 06 and the old paper Figures 2, 4, 5, 6, 7 contain hard-coded or invented numbers (e.g.
  “210.0 / 834.3 / 977.3” limits, “3.56×”, “31.6× volume”, “88 % → 5 % cadence cliff”, “1.87 % quadrature
  agreement”, cartoon light curves labelled as fiesta fits, imposter purity 18.5 % → 84.5 %, elasticity values,
  "649.7 GB / job 14237734" — a seeded record not from sacct).
- All notebooks contained "calibrated" fall-back data that silently replaced missing results.
- *Done:* retired to `defunct/`, notebooks rebuilt to use only snapshot data; real replacements exist for
  convergence (hypergeometric sub-sampling of real runs) and inclination dependence (from the isotropic runs).
  The README and manuscript still quoted these numbers; README rewritten, manuscript **must** be revised (§4).

### 1.4 Metzger models
- Suite 01 Metzger uses κ = 398 cm² g⁻¹, M = 1.26×10⁻³ M☉, v = 0.5c. Physical grey opacities for r-process ejecta
  are ~0.5–30 cm² g⁻¹ (Tanaka+2020). This model is not a kilonova; it is also mislabelled "lanthanide-free".
- Suite 07 "Metzger blue" (M = 0.02, v = 0.25c, κ = 0.5) produces recoveries with median observed peak
  M ≈ −17.6, ~1.6 mag brighter than AT2017gfo (M_r ≈ −16.0), hence its very low R₉₅ = 27. It represents
  only the blue component of a 3-component fit, without the purple/red components. (See §5 for the engine audit.)

*Options:* (a) drop Metzger models from the paper (simplest); (b) fit the one-zone model to AT2017gfo photometry
(or use the Villar+2017 three-component sum) and re-run Suite 07 Metzger only (≈ 2 × 100 × 10⁶ injections, ~30 s
per 10⁶ → cheap); (c) keep as an explicit "bright/blue" bracketing case, clearly labelled.

### 1.5 The "GW170817" Bu2026 parameters are not a published AT2017gfo fit
log M_dyn = −1.8, Ye_dyn = 0.15, log M_wind = −1.1, ι = 0.45 rad (25.8°): ι sits just outside the VLBI range
19–25° (Mooley+2022); Ye_dyn = 0.15 is on the training-range edge; neither Anand+2023 (log M_dyn ≈ −2.87,
log M_wind ≈ −1.32, θ ≈ 28°) nor the fiesta AT2017gfo posterior matches. The S01 and S07 Bu2026 models differ only
in log M_dyn (−1.7 vs −1.8), and give 192 vs 191 (fixed) and 664 vs 624 (isotropic).
*Options:* (a) adopt a published posterior (fiesta/Anand+2023) and state it; (b) **marginalise over the AT2017gfo
posterior** (draw parameters per transient) — the best "AT2017gfo-like" limit; (c) keep but justify.
*Implication:* (b) needs one new run family (≈ 100 × 10⁶ injections, ~2 h each on 1 core per 10⁶).

### 1.6 Population limits do not exist yet (Suite 03 design)
`compare_priors_at2017gfo.py` draws **one** parameter set per 10⁶-injection run (`FixedBu2026KilonovaPopulation`),
so each "BroadPrior" realisation is a different single kilonova (R₉₅ ranging from ~100 to 3.6×10⁵). Masses are drawn
down to log M = −4 and velocities to 0.35c — inside the training box of the deployed `Bu2026_MLP` surrogate
(log M ∈ [−4, −1.30]/[−4, −0.55], v_dyn ≤ 0.35c) but outside the range validated in the fiesta paper (log M_dyn ≥ −3,
log M_wind ≥ −2, v_dyn ≤ 0.28c) and far below typical BNS ejecta masses,
the "FixedView" variant draws a random single inclination instead of the documented 26°, and z_max differs per
population (0.15/0.20/0.25/0.30). The manuscript's "NR population priors" limits came from Suite 05 (synthetic).
*Fix:* per-transient sampling (survey_sim's population class with priors), priors restricted to the training
domain validated in the fiesta paper (or physically motivated NR-informed priors), common z_max chosen from the recovered-redshift distribution, ≥ 10⁷–10⁸
injections. *Implication:* this is the scientifically most interesting limit (a population-averaged kilonova rate)
and is currently missing.

### 1.7 Suite 02 (criteria sweep) bugs
1. Saved candidates contain no position → `gal_lat` defaults to 90° → **|b| cut never applied** (all
   |b|_min give identical results).
2. VT = ε × V_c(z_max; H0=67.7 Taylor series) × 0.6687 × 8.0057 — a hard-coded sky fraction on top of a full-sky
   ε, and a different cosmology from survey_sim (h = 0.674, Ωm = 0.315, Ω = 4π).
3. The Python re-implementation of the criteria is not validated: at the fiducial point ε is 1.59× (Bu2026 fixed)
   and 1.62× (isotropic) the Suite 01 value.
4. Metzger candidates at z_max = 0.30 vs 0.15 elsewhere.
5. README/companion claims (e.g. "N_min ≥ 3 cliff −63 %", "S/N 5→4 doubles VT") are contradicted by the sweep
   itself (−7 % and +12 %).
*Fix:* serialise RA/Dec (or b) per source in survey_sim; normalise VT as VT_loose × N_pass/N_loose (or
survey_sim's per-source weights); validate the filter against Rust at ≥ 5 grid points (identical ε within MC
error) and add that as a unit test; then re-run candidates + filter. *Implication:* criteria-sensitivity results
(companion paper) are not usable until then.

### 1.8 Statistics and comparisons
- Per-run medians of R₉₅ were used for headline numbers; replaced by pooled ⟨VT⟩ (Jensen-unbiased). With ~10⁵
  recoveries per model the difference is < 0.1 % here, but it matters for sparse runs (Suite 03).
- "Duration-scaled" limits (×8.006/1.917) are invalid: ZTF's monthly sensitivity varies by > 2× (seasons,
  shutdowns — e.g. Dec 2021/Jan 2022 contribute almost nothing). Our full survey gives 5.4–5.8× the VT of the
  first 23 months, not 4.18×. Replaced by the measured window limit (notebook 04).
- MC uncertainty is negligible (< 0.5 %); systematics (model, criteria, pipeline completeness) dominate and need
  a budget. Consider quoting Feldman–Cousins (3.09 vs 2.996 at 95 %) or a VT-uncertainty-marginalised limit.
- Cosmology/VT formalism in the manuscript (H0 = 70, Ω_survey/4π, "VT_total = 8.394") does not match the code.

### 1.9 Smaller points
- fiesta is valid for 0.2–26 d; recoveries start at ≥ 0.20 d, so emission before 0.2 d is never used
  (conservative; state it). Isotropic VT in the most polar bin is 1.6× the next bin — use finer bins near the pole
  if quoting angle-resolved limits.
- z_max = 0.15 is safe for all models except Metzger "blue" (max recovered z = 0.148); raise z_max if it is kept.
- The Suite 01 and 07 seeds are unique per run (checked); run-to-run scatter matches binomial noise (checked).
- GW BNS rates: use GWTC-4.0 (7.6–250 Gpc⁻³ yr⁻¹, 90 %); the isotropic Bu2026 limit (624–664) lies above the whole
  range, the fixed-angle limit (191) inside it — "rules out the upper half of the LVK posterior" is not supported.

## 5. survey_sim engine audit (read-only; local runs reproduce cluster outputs bit-for-bit)

How VT is computed (verified): VT = T × 4π × ∫ ε(z) dV/dz /(1+z) dz with full-sky isotropic injection, z ∝ dV/dz,
explosion times uniform over the survey (T = 8.00571 yr), flat ΛCDM h = 0.674, Ωm = 0.315; ε(z) in 20 bins,
trapezoid-integrated. The Galactic-latitude cut is applied after detection on the true position (consistent with 4π).
`volumetric_rate` is bookkeeping only. `vary_inclination` draws cos ι uniformly on [0, 1]. Upper limit 2.996/VT is
consistent with the engine's exact Poisson limit.

Issues ranked by impact on R₉₅ (VERIFIED unless noted):
1. **Pointing over-match, VT ≈ +30 %.** `SpatialIndex::query_cone` (survey-sim `src/spatial/mod.rs:73-99`) accepts
   every exposure whose *centre* lies in any nside-64 pixel touching the 3.87° cone — no exact separation test
   (effective radius inflated by ~0.7°). Same-seed runs at nside 1024 give 0.76× the detections (Bu2026 fixed
   1835 → 1396; Metzger 911 → 691). Fix: exact separation cut, ideally the real per-quadrant footprint (chip gaps).
2. **fiesta time dilation applied twice, Bu2026 VT ≈ +4 %.** fiesta returns observer-frame times but
   `fiesta_model.py` interpolates rest-frame times on that grid (light curves stretched by (1+z)²).
3. **Extinction inconsistent.** Bu2026 (fiesta path) gets none; Metzger gets A_V = 0.02 (MW, constant) + host
   TruncNorm(0.1, 0.2) with A_r/A_V = 1.0. Real MW extinction at |b| > 15° is position dependent (A_V ~ 0.1–0.2
   typical). Estimated ~30 % VT effect (not run). Fix: SFD/Planck E(B−V) at the injected position for all models.
4. **Metzger one-zone model** (lightcurve-fitting `parametric.rs`): Ye fixed at 0.1 (80 % free-neutron mass),
   `kappa` is not the grey opacity (κ_eff ≈ 0.2κ + 0.4·X_n), F_ν lacks a factor π (1.24 mag too faint) and (1+z).
   The Suite 01 fiducial (κ = 398) was grid-fit to AT2017gfo with this formula, i.e. it is an AT2017gfo *template*,
   not lanthanide-free physics; Suite 07 "literature" presets are not calibrated.
5. **No photometric noise in the detection decision or fade slopes** (noise only added to saved photometry).
6. **`min_rise_rate = 0` is not "off"** in Rust (`abs(r) >= 0` always true; rise OR fade passes), +1.3 % VT;
   the Python sweep treats 0 as off and requires rise AND fade when both > 0.
7. **VT integration bias −2 to −5 %** (trapezoid on 20 bins vs. the unbiased MC sum V·T·Σ 1/(1+z_i)/N).
8. **`save_result` rounding** (missing `continue`): rate summaries rounded to 5 decimals; VT < 5×10⁻⁶ becomes 0.
9. Boom cache key weak (file count + summed path length) — risk only.
10. Minor: 14-d timespan cap has no effect at the fiducial; the fade cut removes 24 % of recoveries; the
   `pipeline_time_window_days: 100` metadata in `ZTF_sim.py` is never used (true window: t_exp−30 d to +25(1+z) d).

Suite 02 vs Suite 01 (reproduced with identical populations): the ×1.63 efficiency gap = ×1.30 (|b| cut never
applied) × 1.16 (fade measured from mixed-band consecutive pairs, so a g−r colour offset reads as a fast fade) × ~1.08
(noisy mags, no 2-h dedup, rolling 14-d sub-window). The Python VT/ε is 0.70× the engine's (0.6687 sky-fraction
double count, no 1/(1+z), different V_c).

The deployed `Bu2026_MLP` surrogate's training box is log M_dyn ∈ [−4, −1.301], v_dyn ∈ [0.12, 0.35],
Ye_dyn ∈ [0.15, 0.35], log M_wind ∈ [−4, −0.55], v_wind ∈ [0.05, 0.15], Ye_wind ∈ [0.2, 0.4], ι ∈ [0, π/2],
source-frame 0.194–27.5 d (wider than the fiesta paper's ranges); no clipping outside it. The fiducial Ye_dyn = 0.15
sits on the edge.
